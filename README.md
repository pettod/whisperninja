# Call copilot

Copilot for phone calls

## Setup

### Requirements

iPhone with iOS 8.1 or later

Mac with macOS Yosemite (10.10) or later

Both devices must:

Be signed in to the same Apple ID for iCloud & FaceTime

Be connected to the same Wi-Fi network

Have Bluetooth turned on

### Step 1: Enable Wi-Fi Calling (optional but recommended)

If your carrier supports Wi-Fi Calling, this lets your Mac place calls even when your iPhone is not nearby (e.g., if it's on another floor).

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Phone.

Tap Wi-Fi Calling.

Turn on Wi-Fi Calling on This iPhone.

Go back to Phone → Calls on Other Devices.

Turn on Allow Calls on Other Devices.

Enable your Mac in the list of devices.

### Step 2: Enable Calls on Mac

Open FaceTime on your Mac.

Go to FaceTime → Settings (or Preferences on older versions).

Check ✅ Calls from iPhone.

Step 3: Install [Blackhole](https://existential.audio/blackhole/)
